Not a drop-in replacement

The advantage of SiC and GaN is created in the chip. Whether it reaches the system is decided by the module.

Faster switching edges, higher voltages and power densities shift the critical limits to exactly where standard modules make compromises:

  • Parasitic inductance: voltage overshoot and oscillations
  • Heat dissipation: hot spots limit the usable current
  • Gate drive: false turn-on and ringing at high dv/dt
  • EMC: fast edges create interference

Light‑activated Gate Driving

Our proprietary, patent-pending approach: light replaces the gate bond wires. That removes bond-wire inductance from the gate path and provides inherent galvanic isolation.

  • Up to 50% lower switching losses
  • Up to 60% lower EMI
  • 20 to 30% longer projected lifetime
