Why wide-bandgap

Silicon and wide-bandgap compared

SiliconSiC and GaN
LossesHigher switching and conduction lossesLower losses, higher efficiency
Size and weightLarger passives and coolingMore compact, lighter systems
Operating limitsLower voltage, temperature and frequency limitsHigher voltage, temperature and switching frequency

Qualitative comparison. Actual values depend on device, topology and application.

Wide-bandgap is not a drop-in replacement

The advantage of SiC and GaN is created in the chip. Whether it reaches the system is decided by the module.

Faster switching edges, higher voltages and power densities shift the critical limits to exactly where standard modules make compromises:

  • Parasitic inductances
  • Thermal management
  • Gate drive
  • EMC
