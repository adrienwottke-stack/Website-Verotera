Hard Constraint · E3

Selected rule object

DEMO-02 · Hard Constraint

Condition: Device voltage rating must exceed switch stress including the required margin. For an 800 V bus, a 650 V single switch cannot block the bus in a two-level arrangement.

Consequence: 650 V single-switch two-level candidates are infeasible in this arrangement.

Evidence: E3 · Class envelope and bus voltage, both subject to verification

Version: 0.1-pilot · Pilot values to be verified

Physical calculation models

Voltage stress · Loss estimation · Thermal envelope

Model inputs, derating policies and 12 kW thermal evidence are not validated in this example. No numerical thermal pass is claimed.

Escalation and assessment states
DEMO-05 · Insufficient evidence

Missing bus transient and fault overvoltage profile. Final voltage-stress assessment cannot be completed.
